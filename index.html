The World of Poseidon

A Global Ocean Powered by Geology

Poseidon, a super-Earth with an immense, global ocean, orbits a dim red dwarf star. The lack of significant starlight means that life cannot rely on photosynthesis. Instead, Poseidon's very foundation supports life through a highly active core and thin crust, creating a planet-wide network of intense geothermal heat and constant volcanic activity.

Hydrothermal vents and cold seeps are the "cities" of Poseidon, releasing a rich cocktail of chemical compounds like hydrogen sulfide ($H_2S$), methane ($CH_4$), and dissolved iron ($Fe^{2+}$). These serve as the fundamental energy sources for all life on the planet.

The Biosphere

The Siphonophore Drifters

Resembling colossal, bioluminescent jellyfish, these creatures are composite organisms known as "Siphons." Their bodies contain specialized organs filled with symbiotic bacteria that perform chemosynthesis, processing dissolved chemicals from the water. They communicate through bursts of light and chemical signals in the perpetual darkness.

The Armored Grazers

Slow-moving, crab-like creatures with thick, mineral-plated shells, these "Lithovores" graze on the dense microbial mats surrounding the vents. Their shells, made of calcium carbonate and iron sulfides, provide both formidable protection and a metallic appearance.

The Predatory Hunters

The "Apexes" are the top predators, sleek and agile. They have evolved without eyes, instead using a form of chemosensory echolocation. They send out unique chemical pulses that map their surroundings, allowing them to hunt Siphons and Lithovores with incredible precision in the complete darkness.
